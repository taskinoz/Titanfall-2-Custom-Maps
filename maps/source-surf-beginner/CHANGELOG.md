- Its own loading screen, replacing the Coliseum fallback.
- Map name and description localised for English, French, German, Italian,
  Spanish, Russian, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese, Traditional Chinese and
  Simplified Chinese.
- Pilots stand back up after a teleport.
